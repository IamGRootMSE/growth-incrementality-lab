02 / TARGETING POLICY

Same reach. Different reasons to target.

All strategies face the same held-out users. Read the curves as estimated extra conversions per 1,000 eligible users—not per 1,000 selected users.

Gain above random targeting (Qini)

Subtract the random policy’s gain at each depth. Zero means no estimated advantage over random. This is an unnormalized Qini definition; values are not comparable to every library’s Qini score.

03 / BUDGET LAB

Turn evidence into a scenario.

Explore a hypothetical audience with the benchmark’s response pattern. Costs and conversion values are assumptions, not measured financial results.

Evaluated in 5-point increments. Budget caps reach by rounding down to the nearest evaluated depth. This explorer does not select an optimal policy from test outcomes.

HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

Estimated extra conversions

Net incremental value = extra conversions × assumed value − treated users × assumed cost. This is not total campaign revenue or profit. Intervals propagate benchmark sampling uncertainty only; they exclude uncertainty in costs, value and transport to a new audience.
